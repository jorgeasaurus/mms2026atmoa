Session Title: 
Troubleshooting Application Installation: Who to Blame?

  Description:
Diagnose Intune application installation failures through a systematic approach that identifies whether the issue is with the deployment tool, application, or device. Gain insights into log file analysis and common pitfalls while sharing real-world scenarios. This engaging session balances troubleshooting with humor, preparing attendees to effectively tackle installation challenges with confidence.

What you will learn:
 
* Identify the specific point of failure in the application installation process. 
* Interpret log files to uncover insightful details about installation issues.
* Recognize common errors and learn effective identification techniques.
* Engage with practical experiences and strategies to enhance troubleshooting capabilities.
