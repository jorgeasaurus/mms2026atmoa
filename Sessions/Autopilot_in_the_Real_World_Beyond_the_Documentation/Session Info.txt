Session Title: 
Autopilot in the Real World: Beyond the Documentation

  Description:
Real-world Autopilot deployments reveal complexities often omitted in Microsoft Docs, such as timing issues, app failures, and user experience challenges. Gain insights into configuring effective Autopilot profiles that ensure reliable performance across different hardware vendors and user scenarios. Learn what aspects to prioritize and which configurations to bypass, resulting in a smoother user experience and improved deployment outcomes.

What you will learn:
 
* Understand the critical dependencies of Autopilot during provisioning to enhance reliability. 
* Discover methods to simplify the Enrollment Status Page (ESP) while maintaining control over the process.
* Enhance end-user experiences without compromising on security during deployment.
* Gain actionable insights for configuring Autopilot profiles that ensure consistent performance across various environments.
