Session Title: 
Mastering Intune Driver Management: Insights and Best Practices

  Description:
Discover the intricacies of Intune's driver management solution, addressing the challenges faced by sysadmins in managing over 2 billion drivers. Learn how drivers are submitted to the Microsoft Update catalog, how Intune and Windows select appropriate drivers for devices, and assess the advantages and limitations of the system to ensure it meets organizational needs.

What you will learn:
 
* Learn to configure driver management effectively for your organization. 
* Understand the criteria that Intune and Windows use to select drivers for devices.
* Assess whether Intune's driver management solution aligns with your organization's requirements.
* Identify potential pitfalls and best practices for successful driver management.
