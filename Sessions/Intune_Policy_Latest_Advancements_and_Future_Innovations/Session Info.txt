Session Title: 
Intune Policy: Latest Advancements and Future Innovations

  Description:
Stay ahead with the latest advancements in Microsoft Intune policy impacting modern device management and security. Explore new policy capabilities, including how Microsoft Copilot for Security simplifies configuration and enhances protection for Windows devices. Hear insights from the product team about current innovations, real-world challenges, and a glimpse into the future of Intune policy.

What you will learn:
 
* Understand the latest enhancements to Intune policy and their impact on device management. 
* Explore how Microsoft Copilot for Security streamlines policy configuration and enforcement.
* Learn best practices for using policy to improve security posture across Windows devices.
* Gain insight into upcoming innovations and challenges from the Intune product team.
