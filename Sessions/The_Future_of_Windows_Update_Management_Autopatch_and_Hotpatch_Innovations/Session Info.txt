Session Title: 
The Future of Windows Update Management: Autopatch and Hotpatch Innovations

  Description:
Windows update management is becoming faster, smarter, and more predictable. Delve into the innovations of Windows Autopatch and Hotpatch that are reshaping how organizations maintain security and compliance. Gain insights on current capabilities, future developments, and how these enhancements integrate into the overall Windows update strategy, whether managing thousands of endpoints or planning future updates.

What you will learn:
 
* Understand Autopatch's capabilities and controls for effective Windows update management. 
* Grasp the Hotpatch functionality and build trust in its value proposition for your organization.
* Discover upcoming changes in Update Readiness to better prepare your update strategy.
* Learn integration techniques for Autopatch and Hotpatch within your broader Windows update framework.
