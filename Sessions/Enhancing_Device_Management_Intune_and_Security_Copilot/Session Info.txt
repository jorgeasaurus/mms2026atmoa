Session Title: 
Enhancing Device Management: Intune and Security Copilot

  Description:
Discover the transformative impact of Security Copilot on device management within Microsoft Intune. Leverage AI-driven insights and automation to streamline daily workflows, reduce manual tasks, and expedite troubleshooting. Experience the innovative 'Explorer' capabilities for natural navigation within Intune and gain a sneak peek into forthcoming AI integrations that will redefine endpoint management strategies.

What you will learn:
 
* Understand how Security Copilot integrates with Intune to enhance device management workflows. 
* Explore AI-powered agents that automate common administrative tasks and operations.
* Learn how the new Explorer experience enables intuitive navigation and action within Intune.
* Gain insight into upcoming AI capabilities and how they will impact endpoint management strategies.
