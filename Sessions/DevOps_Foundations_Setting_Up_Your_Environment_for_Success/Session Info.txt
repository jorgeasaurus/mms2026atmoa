Session Title: 
DevOps Foundations: Setting Up Your Environment for Success

  Description:
Establishing a robust foundation is key to any DevOps journey. Learn essential steps for configuring Azure DevOps, including project creation, repositories, service connections, and branch strategies. Gain practical insights that ensure a secure, scalable environment ready for effective collaboration, whether you're new to DevOps or refining your existing setup.

What you will learn:
 
* Understand the core components of Azure DevOps—Projects, Repos, Pipelines, and Service Connections. 
* Learn best practices for branch strategies that foster continual integration and delivery.
* Discover how to configure secure service connections for seamless integration with external resources.
* Gain actionable tips for organizing projects and repositories to enhance team collaboration and maintainability.
