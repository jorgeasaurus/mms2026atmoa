Session Title: 
The Hybrid Update Blues: Orchestrating Patches across Servers, VMs, and Clouds

  Description:
Effective patch management is crucial for maintaining robust architectures. Learn strategies to orchestrate updates seamlessly across Azure Local, Hyper-V clusters, standalone servers, Arc-managed machines, and Azure VMs while avoiding disruptions that could jeopardize your environment’s stability. Equip yourself with actionable techniques to coordinate patching in complex hybrid setups.

What you will learn:
 
* Implement effective update strategies for clusters and Azure Local environments. 
* Explore Arc-enabled update management patterns to enhance patching success.
* Master coordinated patching techniques across hybrid infrastructure environments.
* Discover best practices to avoid update-induced disruptions in your systems.
