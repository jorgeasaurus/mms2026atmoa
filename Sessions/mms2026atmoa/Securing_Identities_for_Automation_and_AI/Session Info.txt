Session Title: 
Securing Identities for Automation and AI

  Description:
As automation and AI operate at machine speed, securing identities in Microsoft Entra (Azure AD) is critical. Learn best practices for managing unattended task identities, covering application vs. dedicated permissions, identity types, and enforcing least privilege. Gain insights into Agent ID in Microsoft Entra and understand how to structure your identity management to stay resilient against evolving threats.

What you will learn:
 
* Gain clarity on authentication, authorization, and permissions context. 
* Discover the essentials of identities, including Users, Service Principals, and Managed Identities.
* Align use cases with identity types for optimized security strategies.
* Prepare for future advancements by connecting workload IDs with AI use cases.
