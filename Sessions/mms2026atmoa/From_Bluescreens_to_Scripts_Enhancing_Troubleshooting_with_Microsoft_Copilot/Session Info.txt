Session Title: 
From Bluescreens to Scripts: Enhancing Troubleshooting with Microsoft Copilot

  Description:
Transform repetitive troubleshooting tasks with Microsoft Copilot, a powerful ally for IT administrators and architects. Learn how to leverage Copilot to generate scripts, analyze event logs, summarize complex error states, and interpret memory dumps—all in natural language tailored to your environment. Streamline your operational efficiency with practical applications directly relevant to daily IT challenges.

What you will learn:
 
* Analyze bluescreen minidumps and receive actionable remediation guidance tailored to your environment. 
* Efficiently parse and summarize noisy event logs to eliminate critical troubleshooting noise.
* Quickly create documentation, reports, and communication artifacts in seconds for faster issue resolution.
* Easily generate PowerShell and automation scripts using simple prompts to enhance productivity.
