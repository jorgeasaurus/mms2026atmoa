Session Title: 
Prompt Engineering: Mastering the Craft Two Years Later

  Description:
Sharpen your prompt engineering skills by learning to ask smarter questions, structure context, and tailor prompts for chat, retrieval, and workflow agents. Discover effective patterns, anti-patterns, and safety measures through engaging demos and take-home templates designed for rapid deployment of reliable agents. Experience an exciting AI 'boss fight' to put your newfound skills to the test.

What you will learn:
 
* Review diverse prompt patterns and techniques for optimizing performance across different AI engines. 
* Gain hands-on experience building a straightforward M365 agent tailored to your needs.
* Uncover strategies to maximize the potential of Teams Premium and M365 Copilot tools.
* Access essential take-home templates for efficient agent deployment and improved workflows.
