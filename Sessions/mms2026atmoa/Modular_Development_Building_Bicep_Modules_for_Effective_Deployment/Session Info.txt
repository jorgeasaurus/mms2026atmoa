Session Title: 
Modular Development: Building Bicep Modules for Effective Deployment

  Description:
Achieve consistency and reusability in cloud deployments by creating modular Bicep templates stored in an Azure Container Registry. Integrate these modules into Azure DevOps pipelines to enable standardized deployments and minimize duplication. Streamline your infrastructure-as-code practices and enhance maintainability with key development principles and actionable strategies for effective module management.

What you will learn:
 
* Understand principles of modular development with Bicep for scalable infrastructure-as-code solutions. 
* Learn to build and publish Bicep modules to an Azure Container Registry for centralized management and reuse.
* Discover integration techniques for utilizing shared modules across multiple projects and repositories in Azure DevOps.
* Gain best practices for versioning and governance to ensure deployment consistency and reliability.
