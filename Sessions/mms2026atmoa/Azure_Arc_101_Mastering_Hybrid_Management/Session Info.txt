Session Title: 
Azure Arc 101: Mastering Hybrid Management

  Description:
Navigating hybrid and multi-cloud environments presents unique challenges for resource management. Azure Arc empowers organizations by extending Azure's management and governance to servers, Kubernetes, and databases, whether on-premises, at the edge, or in other clouds. Discover the fundamentals of Azure Arc and unlock its full potential for consistent, secure resource management.

What you will learn:
 
* Understand how the Azure control plane enables consistent management across on-premises, edge, and multi-cloud environments. 
* Learn to onboard servers to Azure Arc and assess your environment swiftly with built-in insights and inventory views.
* Manage Arc-enabled servers at scale with ease—apply updates, troubleshoot, monitor health, and perform key operations directly from the Azure Portal.
* Explore best practices for implementing Azure Arc to enhance visibility and control across diverse infrastructures.
