Session Title: 
Hello Passkey, Bye Password: Phishing-Resistant MFA Made Simple

  Description:
Transition from traditional passwords to modern passwordless, phishing-resistant MFA with Windows Hello for Business (WHfB). Learn to configure and deploy WHfB alongside Microsoft Intune, ensuring centralized management and integration into endpoint and identity strategies. Understand the advantages of adopting this advanced authentication method while exploring best practices and prerequisites for effective implementation.

What you will learn:
 
* Understand the security and usability benefits of moving beyond passwords to phishing-resistant authentication. 
* Master the management of WHfB in contemporary IT environments for enhanced security.
* Explore the integration of WHfB within endpoint and identity management for a seamless Zero Trust approach.
* Gain insights into best practices and prerequisites for configuring WHfB alongside Microsoft Intune.
