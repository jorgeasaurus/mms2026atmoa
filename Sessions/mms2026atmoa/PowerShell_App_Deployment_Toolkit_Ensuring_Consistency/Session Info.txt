Session Title: 
PowerShell App Deployment Toolkit: Ensuring Consistency

  Description:
Encountering outdated software installers can be frustrating, but the PowerShell App Deployment Toolkit (PSADT) offers a standardized approach for app installation and removal. This toolkit provides extensive options for managing legacy applications, including removing older versions and applying customizations to fit your environment. With its readable PowerShell foundation, ensure a consistent install experience without the headaches typically associated with antiquated software.

What you will learn:
 
* Learn to get started with the PowerShell App Deployment Toolkit (PSADT) efficiently. 
* Achieve consistent application installations and removals across diverse environments.
* Say goodbye to cumbersome service UIs through streamlined automation.
* Unlock customization options to adapt installations to specific organizational needs.
