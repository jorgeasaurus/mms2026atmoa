Session Title: 
Inside the Intune Management Extension: Troubleshooting App Installation Failures

  Description:
Examine the Intune Management Extension (IME) app installation process, tracing each step from the SideCar AppWorkload activation to content execution and detection. Identify critical pain points that lead to failures or delays, including the notorious sixty-minute wait. This session uses real logs and thorough reverse engineering of the IME to uncover common issues and solutions, even for unsupported scenarios, empowering IT pros to troubleshoot effectively.

What you will learn:
 
* Gain a clear understanding of the IME app installation workflow for effective troubleshooting. 
* Identify common failure points where application installations can go awry.
* Learn actionable solutions to installation issues, even in scenarios not officially supported by Microsoft.
* Access real-world log analysis and reverse engineering insights to enhance your Intune management skills.
