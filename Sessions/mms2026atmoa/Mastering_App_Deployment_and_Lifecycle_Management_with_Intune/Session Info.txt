Session Title: 
Mastering App Deployment and Lifecycle Management with Intune

  Description:
Effectively deploy and manage applications using Microsoft Intune with a focus on best practices. Discover techniques for quick, efficient deployment of Microsoft and third-party apps while steering clear of common pitfalls. Gain insights on app lifecycle management, including patching, updating, and timely removal of apps. Acquire the tools and strategies necessary for successful deployment whether scaling operations or handling individual updates.

What you will learn:
 
* Best practices for deploying Microsoft and third-party apps seamlessly using Intune. 
* Integrate Intune with Windows for a streamlined app delivery process.
* Implement techniques for efficiently patching and updating deployed applications.
* Learn to avoid common pitfalls in app deployment and application management.
