Session Title: 
Inside Intune: Internals, Diagnostics, and Troubleshooting

  Description:
Dive into the inner workings of Intune, uncovering data flow, policy processing, and state reporting beyond the portal. Acquire practical troubleshooting skills for issues related to enrollment, policies, compliance, and app deployment through logs and built-in diagnostics. Focus on real-world scenarios and repeatable approaches that can be implemented immediately in production environments.

What you will learn:
 
* Gain a comprehensive understanding of Intune's data flow and processing mechanisms. 
* Learn effective techniques for troubleshooting policies, applications, and compliance issues.
* Apply repeatable methods to tackle real-world Intune problems efficiently.
* Enhance diagnostic skills using logs and built-in tools for swift issue resolution.
