Session Title: 
What’s New in Mobility Management with Microsoft Intune

  Description:
Discover the latest enhancements in Microsoft Intune for managing mobile devices and applications on iOS and Android. Review new features that streamline mobile device management (MDM) and mobile application management (MAM), bolstering security and enhancing user experience. Gain insights into upcoming capabilities that will modernize and strengthen your organization’s mobility strategy, ensuring you stay ahead in today’s dynamic mobile landscape.

What you will learn:
 
* Understand the latest Intune enhancements for managing iOS and Android devices effectively. 
* Explore new mobile application management (MAM) capabilities and practical use cases.
* Learn how recent updates enhance security and control over mobile endpoints.
* Identify upcoming features and roadmap items that will shape your mobility strategy.
