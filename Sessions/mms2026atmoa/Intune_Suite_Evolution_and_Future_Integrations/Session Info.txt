Session Title: 
Intune Suite: Evolution and Future Integrations

  Description:
Discover the evolution of the Microsoft Intune Suite and its future impact on endpoint management. Gain insights into the product roadmap, upcoming integrations, and strategic changes affecting organizational adoption. Engage directly with the engineering team to voice your feedback and play a role in shaping the Intune ecosystem’s direction.

What you will learn:
 
* Understand current capabilities and the evolving role of the Intune Suite in endpoint management. 
* Explore upcoming integrations and roadmap items that will shape the platform's future.
* Learn how changes to the suite affect licensing, positioning, and adoption strategies within organizations.
* Engage with the product team, offering feedback to influence future development of the Intune ecosystem.
Provide Feedback
