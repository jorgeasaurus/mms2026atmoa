Session Title: 
Microsoft Edge Management Service: Modern Cross-Platform Browser Management

  Description:
As web-based applications dominate, browser management takes on heightened importance. Discover the transformative Microsoft Edge management service, enabling efficient management of settings and extension requests across multiple platforms, including Windows and Google Chrome. This session offers a hands-on demo on securing and configuring Edge, highlighting critical mistakes to avoid for effective browser management.

What you will learn:
 
* Gain insights from real-world examples of Microsoft Edge management in action. 
* Learn to leverage the Edge Management Service for efficient cross-platform browser management.
* Identify valuable lessons learned for optimizing Edge security and configuration.
* Understand best practices to avoid common pitfalls in browser management.
