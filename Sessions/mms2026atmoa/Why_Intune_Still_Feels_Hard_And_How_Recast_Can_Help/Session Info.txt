Session Title: 
Why Intune Still Feels Hard (And How Recast Can Help)

  Description:
Leverage Recast to manage Intune and co-managed devices with enhanced efficiency. Discover Right Click Tools tailored for Intune, Entra, and Autopilot, enabling application patching, full-lifecycle app management, and real-time endpoint visibility. These tools streamline deployment tracking, reduce operational complexity, and enhance visibility in co-managed environments.

What you will learn:
 
* Deploy applications effortlessly from a comprehensive and managed catalog without the need for repackaging. 
* Gain greater control over third-party app compliance with customizable testing rings, rollout strategies, and update timing.
* Achieve real-time visibility across co-managed environments, resolving issues seamlessly without console hopping.
* Expand Right Click Tools' capabilities beyond Intune and ConfigMgr to any web-based platform, including Power BI and ServiceNow.
