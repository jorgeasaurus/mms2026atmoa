Session Title: 
Intune and ConfigMgr: Business-Driven Co-Management

  Description:
Manage Azure AD-only and hybrid devices seamlessly with Co-Management. Dispel the myth that Azure AD-only environments eliminate ConfigMgr options and discover what is truly possible. Equip yourself with insights to make informed decisions tailored to business requirements, enhancing your organization’s management strategy and operational efficiency.

What you will learn:
 
* Implement flexible targeting strategies for Intune and ConfigMgr policies. 
* Execute real-time actions such as 'Run Scripts', 'CMPivot', install applications, and collect logs.
* Leverage integrated and customizable inventory and reporting capabilities.
* Gain insights to help make informed decisions based on specific business needs.
Provide Feedback
