Session Title: 
Optimize Your Azure Monitor Costs: Efficient Data Management

  Description:
Minimize monitoring expenses in Azure by implementing effective strategies for data collection and retention. Explore various filtering techniques to eliminate unnecessary data and evaluate storage tiers within your Log Analytics workspace. Receive practical recommendations on data types, their storage destinations, and functionalities to achieve optimal efficiency without overspending, ensuring all essential monitoring capabilities are maintained.

What you will learn:
 
* Discover filtering techniques in Azure Monitor ranging from basic to advanced, enhancing your cost efficiency. 
* Understand how different storage tiers in a Log Analytics workspace impact functionality and costs.
* Receive tailored recommendations on data filtering and appropriate storage destinations for various data types.
* Learn practical strategies to maintain essential monitoring capabilities while controlling Azure costs effectively.
