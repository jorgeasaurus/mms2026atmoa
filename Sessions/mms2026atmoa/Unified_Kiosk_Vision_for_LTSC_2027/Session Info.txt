Session Title: 
Unified Kiosk: Vision for LTSC 2027

  Description:
Discover Windows Unified Kiosk Mode, a streamlined and secure solution that eliminates fragmentation while simplifying deployment. This approach supports multi-app scenarios, enhances modern management via Intune and PowerShell, and ensures a consistent user experience across all Windows editions.

What you will learn:
 
* Unified Configuration: A single workflow simplifies multiple kiosk modes, reducing complexity and boosting scalability. 
* Modern Management: Leverage a brand new Kiosk CSP and PowerShell cmdlets for straightforward setup, diagnostics, and advanced customization.
* Customer Benefits: Experience improved reliability, security, and flexibility for multi-user and multi-app scenarios, perfect for retail, healthcare, and public services.
* Opportunities to support and participate in the insider preview scheduled for the second half of 2026.
