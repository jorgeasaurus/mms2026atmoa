Session Title: 
AI Orchestration: Transforming Human Work

  Description:
Explore the architecture of orchestrated AI systems that manage complexity in real-world environments. Delve into the coordination of models, tools, memory, and decision loops essential for effective production-level performance. Gain insights into what differentiates toy agents from functional software, backed by tangible lessons and practical mental models for implementing reliable AI solutions.

What you will learn:
 
* Understand why relying solely on prompting is insufficient for production-grade AI implementation. 
* Learn how to effectively coordinate models, tools, memory, and workflows for optimized performance.
* Identify patterns that enhance the reliability and utility of AI systems in diverse environments.
* Differentiate between real architectures that deliver results and demo theater that lacks substance.
