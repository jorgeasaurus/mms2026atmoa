Session Title: 
AI Fundamentals: Demystifying Artificial Intelligence

  Description:
Understand the essentials of AI through clear explanations and live examples that make complex concepts approachable. Explore different types of AI, common terminology, and the underlying technologies driving modern systems. Gain a solid foundation in key topics like machine learning and neural networks, empowering you to engage confidently in AI discussions and deepen your knowledge. This session is perfect for newcomers and those seeking to fill knowledge gaps.

What you will learn:
 
* Gain a clear understanding of key AI concepts such as machine learning, neural networks, and generative models. 
* Differentiate between narrow AI, general AI, agents, operators, and various common categories.
* Receive practical explanations of the technologies powering modern AI, from training data to inference mechanisms.
* Build confidence to discuss AI accurately, enabling you to ask deeper questions in future discussions.
