Session Title: 
Mastering Microsoft 365 Licensing

  Description:
Navigate the complexities of Microsoft 365 licensing to enhance productivity while ensuring compliance. Understand the foundational role licensing plays in implementing proper controls within your tenant. Explore which SKUs to avoid, discover new features included in existing licenses, and learn strategic product choices that maximize budgetary efficiency while extending security protections.

What you will learn:
 
* Discover new features available within your existing Microsoft 365 licenses. 
* Understand the specific rules governing each main license type for informed decisions.
* Identify key indicators of how Microsoft alters SKUs and product offerings.
* Uncover strategies to optimize product choices for better budget utilization and enhanced protections.
