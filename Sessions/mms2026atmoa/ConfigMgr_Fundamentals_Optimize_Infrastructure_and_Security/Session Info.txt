Session Title: 
ConfigMgr Fundamentals: Optimize Infrastructure and Security

  Description:
Enhance ConfigMgr skills by focusing on infrastructure planning, security management, and maintaining system health. Attendees will gain critical knowledge to optimize performance and security, ensuring a resilient ConfigMgr environment. Dive into best practices for effective resource management, troubleshooting common issues, and implementing robust security measures to strengthen your ConfigMgr expertise.

What you will learn:
 
* Identify and troubleshoot common infrastructure challenges within your ConfigMgr environment. 
* Implement effective security measures to manage access and protect sensitive information.
* Develop strategies for continuously monitoring and maintaining infrastructure health.
* Explore best practices for optimizing your ConfigMgr setup to enhance overall efficiency.
