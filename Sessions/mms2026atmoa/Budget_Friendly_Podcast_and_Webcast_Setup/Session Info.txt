Session Title: 
Budget-Friendly Podcast and Webcast Setup

   [https://github.com/OSDeploy/mmsmoa26-podcast-webcast](https://github.com/OSDeploy/mmsmoa26-podcast-webcast)

Description:
Create a professional podcast and webcast setup without overspending. Learn to select cost-effective cameras, microphones, lighting, and software while optimizing your environment for exceptional audio and video quality. Discover essential techniques for acoustics and setup to achieve the best results within any budget. Equip yourself with a compact, portable solution ideal for taking your production on the road to conferences and events.

What you will learn:
 
* Select affordable, quality equipment for audio, video, and lighting tailored to your budget tier and content goals. 
* Optimize your recording space using practical treatment techniques to maximize audio and video quality without breaking the bank.
* Design a portable podcast and webcast setup for seamless transport to events and conferences.
* Access tips for expanding your podcasting capabilities while maintaining budget-friendly practices.
