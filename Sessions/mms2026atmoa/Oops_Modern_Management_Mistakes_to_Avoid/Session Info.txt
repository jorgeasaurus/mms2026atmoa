Session Title: 
Oops! Modern Management Mistakes to Avoid

  Description:
Every IT professional encounters mistakes that vary from minor misconfigurations to significant disruptions impacting the entire organization. Analyze real-world scenarios of misconfigured policies, forgotten monitoring, failed migrations, and hasty PowerShell commands. Understand what went wrong and learn strategies to prevent these pitfalls from recurring in your environment.

What you will learn:
 
* Identify common management, security, and cloud implementation mistakes to avoid future pitfalls. 
* Discover best practices for checks, tools, and processes that minimize risks in IT management.
* Engage in scenario analysis to explore concrete cases and discuss alternative approaches.
* Gain actionable strategies to implement immediately within your own IT environment.
