Session Title: 
Polishing Scripts with AI for Community Sharing

  Description:
Transform functional yet messy scripts into polished, shareable resources using AI tools. Learn to clean and standardize your code, generate clear documentation, enhance the user interface, and convert personal scripts into community-friendly solutions effortlessly. Discover how to make your technical work accessible and appealing to a wider audience while ensuring that the integrity and functionality remain intact, allowing for seamless collaboration in the tech community.

What you will learn:
 
* Leverage AI tools to clean and standardize your scripts for broader usability. 
* Automatically generate clear documentation and helpful comments for your code.
* Enhance your scripts with polished UI elements, even when not strictly necessary.
* Transform personal, one-off scripts into community-ready solutions with minimal effort.
