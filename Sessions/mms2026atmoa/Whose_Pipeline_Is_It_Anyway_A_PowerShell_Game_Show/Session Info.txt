Session Title: 
Whose Pipeline Is It Anyway? A PowerShell Game Show

  Description:
Engage in a thrilling game of PowerShell trivia, where participants tackle multiple-choice questions about the intricacies and quirks of the language. Each answer reveals the reasoning behind it, delivering bite-sized lessons that sharpen your understanding of PowerShell and illuminate those often-overlooked aspects of the pipeline. Whether you're an experienced scripter or there for the fun, expect to leave with new insights and maybe a prize or two.

What you will learn:
 
* Gain a rapid-fire understanding of script block interpretation through engaging trivia. 
* Discover quirky and fascinating facts about the PowerShell language that elevate your expertise.
* Refresh your memory on essential concepts in PowerShell that are frequently overlooked.
* Have a chance to win prizes while enhancing your PowerShell knowledge in a fun-filled atmosphere.
