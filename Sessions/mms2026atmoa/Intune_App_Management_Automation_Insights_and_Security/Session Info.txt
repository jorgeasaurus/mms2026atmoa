Session Title: 
Intune App Management: Automation, Insights, and Security

  Description:
Managing applications in the Windows ecosystem can be complex, involving acquisition, packaging, deployment, and updates. Discover the latest enhancements in Intune app management and patching aimed at streamlining operations while ensuring a secure, up-to-date application landscape. Gain practical guidance and refreshed content that can be implemented immediately to modernize and future-proof your application lifecycle management.

What you will learn:
 
* Discover the latest advancements in Intune app management and patching. 
* Learn about the critical importance of effective application lifecycle management.
* Explore tools and products designed to enhance your app lifecycle management.
* Receive actionable strategies to simplify operations and ensure security in your app landscape.
