Session Title: 
Taming macOS Management with Microsoft Intune

  Description:
Transform macOS management for Windows-focused admins using Microsoft Intune's modern capabilities. Gain insights into best practices and real-world scenarios while leveraging Platform SSO for improved user experiences. Learn how third-party tools can enhance Intune, enabling custom policies and configurations that exceed native functionalities. Streamline Mac control and elevate your IT management strategy.

What you will learn:
 
* Navigate the complete lifecycle of macOS management from deployment to retirement. 
* Discover how Platform SSO enhances the user experience for macOS administration.
* Access real-world examples of effective configurations and tailored policies.
* Explore best practices that elevate macOS management to match Windows capabilities.
