Session Title: 
Preserving Digital Assets: A Practical Guide

  Description:
Address the often-overlooked reality of digital asset preservation in the face of death. Explore strategies to secure your digital belongings, share access with loved ones, and consider items often forgotten. Gain insights from individuals who've navigated digital asset recovery, leaving with actionable steps to safeguard your assets and support others. This important yet approachable topic combines pragmatism and humor while fostering essential discussions over your favorite drinks.

What you will learn:
 
* Gain a comprehensive understanding of your digital assets' scope and significance. 
* Develop your own digital asset preservation plan tailored to your needs.
* Learn secure practices for timing the sharing of digital assets with survivors.
* Receive firsthand tips and insights from individuals experienced in digital asset recovery.
