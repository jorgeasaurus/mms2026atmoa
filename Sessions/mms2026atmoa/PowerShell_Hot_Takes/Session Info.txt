Session Title: 
PowerShell Hot Takes

  Description:
Navigate the complex landscape of PowerShell with a critical examination of widely held beliefs and contemporary practices. Uncover why there's no universal approach to writing PowerShell scripts, highlighting varying expert opinions on debugging, error handling, performance optimization, and GUI/UI implementation, helping attendees navigate through outdated practices and gain insights into modern methodologies.

What you will learn:
 
* Explore different techniques for debugging and effective error handling in PowerShell scripts. 
* Understand various perspectives on performance optimization to enhance script efficiency.
* Examine diverse opinions surrounding GUI/UI implementation for better user experience.
* Gain insights into modern PowerShell practices to elevate your scripting skills.
