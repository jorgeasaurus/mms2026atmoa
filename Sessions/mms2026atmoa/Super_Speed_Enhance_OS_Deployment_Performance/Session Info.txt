Session Title: 
Super-Speed: Enhance OS Deployment Performance

  Description:
Gain insights from Johan Arwidmark and Mikael Nystrom on accelerating OS deployment processes through expert tips and tricks. Discover techniques to optimize images, streamline driver and application deployments, and enhance infrastructure performance. Learn practical methods for peer-to-peer (P2P) distribution and effective image management, ensuring faster deployments across your organization. Leave with actionable strategies to elevate your deployment efficiency.

What you will learn:
 
* Learn strategies to significantly improve OS deployment performance and efficiency. 
* Explore best practices for optimizing driver and application deployments during OS installations.
* Understand how to build a robust infrastructure for enhancing imaging and large deployments.
* Implement peer-to-peer (P2P) techniques for quicker and more efficient software distribution.
