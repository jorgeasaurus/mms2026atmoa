Session Title: 
No Code, Big Impact: Harnessing Copilot Studio for Admin Tasks

  Description:
Transform AI hype into practical solutions with Microsoft Copilot Studio. Gain insights into its functionalities, connection to your environment, and utilization of connectors to manage and access data—all without writing a single line of code. Streamline support tasks with simple, actionable steps and explore the potential of Copilot-powered agents to inspire IT professionals in enhancing their workflows.

What you will learn:
 
* Lower the barrier to AI entry with simple strategies using Copilot Studio. 
* Leverage low-code integration with Power Platform for seamless development.
* Scale Copilot Studio agents through Microsoft MCP Server and custom connectors.
* Discover practical examples of creating agents to assist IT professionals with daily tasks.
