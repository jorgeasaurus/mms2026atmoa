Session Title: 
Beyond the Patch: Uncovering Intune Compliance Risks

  Description:
Patching and enforcing compliance may feel like a victory, but hidden vulnerabilities threaten your security. Explore advanced attack vectors including credential theft and lateral movement while examining how endpoint management, identity security, and Microsoft Defender XDR integrate to strengthen defenses in modern cloud environments. This session dives deep into enhancing compliance strategies for better security outcomes.

What you will learn:
 
* Understand the limitations of compliance-focused security, revealing why patched devices may still pose risks. 
* Combine Microsoft Defender XDR, Conditional Access, and Zero Trust Principles to establish a robust defense against evolving threats.
* Implement practical steps for Zero Trust principles and utilize Microsoft Defender XDR to proactively mitigate threats in cloud settings.
* Identify key integrations between endpoint management and security technologies for enhanced protection in cloud environments.
