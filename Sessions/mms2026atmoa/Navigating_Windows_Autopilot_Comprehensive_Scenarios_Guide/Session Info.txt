Session Title: 
Navigating Windows Autopilot: Comprehensive Scenarios Guide

  Description:
Dive into the expanded landscape of Windows Autopilot with insights into both Autopilot v1 and the new v2 (Device Preparation). Review the complete array of scenarios, learn setup processes, and discover strategies to address gaps and limitations effectively. This session equips attendees with solutions to maximize Autopilot's potential for their environments.

What you will learn:
 
* Gain a clear understanding of available Autopilot scenarios along with their limitations. 
* Learn precise steps for implementing various Autopilot scenarios in your environment.
* Discover effective methods to work around existing limitations in Autopilot.
* Access valuable strategies for enhancing your deployment using the Autopilot framework.
