Session Title: 
Drift Elimination: Mastering Intune Security Baselines and CIS Benchmarks

  Description:
Configuration drift poses a significant threat to enterprise security, often due to relaxed default settings and inconsistent policies. Master Microsoft Intune security baselines alongside CIS Benchmarks Level 1 and Level 2 for a robust defense. Engage in live demos that explore the latest Intune baselines for Windows 10/11, Microsoft Edge, and Microsoft 365 Apps while gaining practical insights for customization, conflict avoidance, and production-safe rollouts.

What you will learn:
 
* Deploy and customize the latest Intune security baselines for Windows 10/11, Microsoft Edge, and Microsoft 365 Apps with live demonstrations of versioning and conflict avoidance. 
* Compare and map CIS Benchmarks Level 1 and Level 2 to Intune policies, identifying enhancements over Microsoft defaults and implementation strategies.
* Utilize official CIS assessment templates and tools to document configurations, track deviations, and prepare audit-ready compliance evidence.
* Gain insights into effective, production-safe rollout strategies for Intune policies to mitigate security risks and ensure compliance.
