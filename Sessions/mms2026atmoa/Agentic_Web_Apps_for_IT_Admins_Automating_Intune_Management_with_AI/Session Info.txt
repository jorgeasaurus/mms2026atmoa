Session Title: 
Agentic Web Apps for IT Admins: Automating Intune Management with AI

  Description:
Streamline Intune device and application management by building Agentic Web Applications that utilize AI-driven workflows. Simplify compliance, automate patching, and optimize app deployments through conversational commands and context-aware agents. Transform IT operations into smarter, faster, and more efficient processes by harnessing the power of intelligent automation.

What you will learn:
 
* Grasp the significance of Agentic Web Apps and their value for IT Admins. 
* Integrate Intune APIs and Windows Autopatch APIs into effective AI-driven workflows.
* Learn best practices for designing secure, scalable agentic apps tailored for enterprise needs.
* Develop your own automation app for compliance remediation and efficient app deployment.
