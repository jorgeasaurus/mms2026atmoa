Session Title: 
Ensuring Business Continuity: Challenges in Modern Endpoint Management

  Description:
A robust and scalable strategy for endpoint recovery is essential for organizations to minimize downtime and streamline manual interventions. Examine the critical role of endpoint recovery and business continuity in combating cyber threats while adhering to regulatory standards. Uncover modern strategies and best practices to effectively recover Windows-based devices amidst evolving challenges in today’s digital landscape.

What you will learn:
 
* Integrate endpoint devices into overall business recovery plans for enhanced resilience. 
* Align endpoint disaster recovery efforts with business continuity and regulatory compliance.
* Discover tailored disaster recovery strategies specific to ‘modern’ Windows endpoints.
* Explore the options and challenges associated with device recovery in modern endpoint management.
