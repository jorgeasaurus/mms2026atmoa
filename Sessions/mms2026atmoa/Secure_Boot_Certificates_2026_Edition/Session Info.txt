Session Title: 
Secure Boot Certificates: 2026 Edition

  Description:
Gain insights into the evolving landscape of Black Lotus remediations with a focus on KB5025885. Learn effective methods for updating Secure Boot to mitigate CVE-2023-24932. Whether new to Black Lotus or advancing your strategies, this session equips you with essential information to ensure a successful implementation phase.

What you will learn:
 
* Identify various methods for applying the latest Black Lotus remediations successfully. 
* Explore efficient testing and deployment strategies to ensure smooth transitions.
* Learn best practices for managing boot media, recovery media, and PXE booting.
* Understand how to enforce revocations and maintain a steady state post-remediation.
