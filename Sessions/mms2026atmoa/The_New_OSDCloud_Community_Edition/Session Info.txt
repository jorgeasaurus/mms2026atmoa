Session Title: 
The New OSDCloud Community Edition

  OSDCloud has crossed two million cloud Windows deployments, and the Community Edition is just getting started. New tools for the OSD Administrator will be released at this session -- including Hydra, a single-command deployment lab builder; OSDCloud Explorer, a new way to navigate and manage your environment from WinPE; and a profile-based WinPE Startup experience that makes boot-time configuration repeatable and portable. These additions are built directly from community feedback and designed to reduce the gap between a clean machine and a running OS. Come ready to be surprised -- the full reveal happens live in the room.

What you will learnDiscover what's new in the OSDCloud Community Edition and how it changes your deployment workflow.Automate the full deployment workstation and boot image build process from a single command.Extend OSDCloud integration with the tools already in your environment.Run a complete OS deployment end-to-end using the latest OSDCloud capabilities.
