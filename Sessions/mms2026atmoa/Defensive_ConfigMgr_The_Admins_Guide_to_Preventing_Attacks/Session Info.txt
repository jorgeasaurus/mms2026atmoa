Session Title: 
Defensive ConfigMgr: The Admin's Guide to Preventing Attacks

  Description:
ConfigMgr is often a primary target for adversaries due to its capability to execute arbitrary programs on client devices. This session reveals common misconfigurations within ConfigMgr that attackers exploit, while providing step-by-step guidance to remediate these vulnerabilities. Learn how to secure your system effectively, reduce security risks, and ensure your ConfigMgr deployment is robust, reducing the likelihood of breaches.

What you will learn:
 
* Gain insight into commonly abused ConfigMgr misconfigurations that pose security risks. 
* Watch demonstrations of attacks targeting ConfigMgr servers and clients.
* Receive step-by-step guidance to remediate vulnerabilities in ConfigMgr.
* Learn strategies to secure your ConfigMgr deployment, minimizing potential security breaches.
