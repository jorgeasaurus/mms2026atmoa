Session Title: 
Day One Essentials for Microsoft Intune Success

  Description:
Organizations often deploy Microsoft Intune without laying the groundwork, leading to complications later. Focus on pivotal day-one decisions essential for both new and existing tenants. Learn critical configurations that should be prioritized, common pitfalls to avoid, and how to effectively devise a practical checklist for immediate application, ensuring a smooth Intune implementation from the outset.

What you will learn:
 
* Gain insights into the essential Intune configurations needed on day one for successful deployment. 
* Identify and sidestep common mistakes that can hinder early-stage Intune setups.
* Utilize a practical day-one checklist for efficient configuration management.
* Leave with a clear, prioritized approach for immediate application in your Intune strategy.
