Session Title: 
Hidden Secrets of the PowerShell Masters

  Description:
Delve into the hidden secrets of PowerShell mastery, focusing on advanced troubleshooting techniques, uncovering and utilizing hidden .NET methods, mastering classes, and optimizing event-driven processes. Transform your PowerShell capabilities to streamline efficiency and enhance the performance of your scripts through best practices and effective organization.

What you will learn:
 
* Discover methods to significantly enhance efficiency in your PowerShell scripts. 
* Learn techniques for improving script organization and maintaining cleanliness.
* Implement effective troubleshooting and debugging approaches to resolve issues.
* Adopt performance optimization tips and established best practices for PowerShell.
