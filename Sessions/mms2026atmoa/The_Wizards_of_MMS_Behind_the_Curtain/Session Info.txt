Session Title: 
The Wizards of MMS: Behind the Curtain

  Description:
Discover the inner workings of MMS and its automation framework. Understand how Azure services power the backend processes, including email delivery systems and Discord role assignments. Gain insights into choosing appropriate Azure services for similar tasks and learn to make informed decisions when faced with comparable options. This session demystifies the technical aspects that keep MMS running smoothly.

What you will learn:
 
* Explore how Azure services streamline and automate key MMS functionalities. 
* Identify which Azure services can be leveraged for tasks similar to those in MMS.
* Learn criteria for selecting the right Azure service among similar options.
* Gain confidence in utilizing Azure's capabilities for automation and efficiency.
