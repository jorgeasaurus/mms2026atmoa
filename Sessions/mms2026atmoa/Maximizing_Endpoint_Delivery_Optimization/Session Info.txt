Session Title: 
Maximizing Endpoint Delivery Optimization

  Description:
Dive into the latest strategies and recommendations for leveraging Delivery Optimization in Intune and beyond. Discover recent changes, effective configuration tips, and community-driven tools and scripts. This comprehensive session equips endpoint admins with essential knowledge to streamline modern content delivery efforts.

What you will learn:
 
* Stay updated on the latest advancements in Modern Content Delivery techniques. 
* Master the optimal configuration methods for Windows Delivery Optimization.
* Acquire valuable tips and tricks to enhance your delivery processes.
* Utilize community tools and scripts for effective endpoint management.
