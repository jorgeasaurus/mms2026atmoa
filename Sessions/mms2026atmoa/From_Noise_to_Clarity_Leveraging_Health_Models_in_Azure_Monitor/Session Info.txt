Session Title: 
From Noise to Clarity: Leveraging Health Models in Azure Monitor

  Description:
Transform application management with the new Health Models feature in Azure Monitor. Visualize components of your Azure applications while assessing their health at a glance. Learn to create alerts for degraded application health rather than minor issues, using signals derived from metrics and logs to enhance your monitoring strategy effectively. Discover streamlined methods for establishing efficient alerting based on the health relationships of components.

What you will learn:
 
* Model application health effectively by analyzing components and their interrelationships. 
* Utilize multiple signals derived from metrics and logs to accurately measure component health.
* Develop an alerting strategy focused on overall application health rather than individual metrics.
* Implement efficient monitoring solutions and leverage Health Models for better application insight.
