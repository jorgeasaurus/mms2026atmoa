Session Title: 
Windows Update Internals: Why Didn't I Get That Update?

  Description:
Understand the intricacies of Windows Update to troubleshoot why certain updates fail to appear for devices. Dive into the evaluation of metadata, applicability, and supersedence to grasp how Windows Update determines which updates to deliver. Learn how Intune, Autopatch, WSUS, and ConfigMgr influence or modify this decision-making process, providing insights into effective update management for enhanced device performance.

What you will learn:
 
* Trace the update flow from the Microsoft Update catalog to Windows devices for better understanding. 
* Employ practical heuristics to diagnose instances of missing or 'not applicable' updates effectively.
* Learn how WSUS and ConfigMgr alter native Windows Update behavior for optimal device performance.
* Discover how Intune and Autopatch enhance and sometimes override Windows Update logic.
