Session Title: 
Enhancing Intune Reporting with Log Analytics and Workbooks

  Description:
Maximize Intune reporting capabilities through the integration of Log Analytics and Workbooks. Utilize Kusto Query Language (KQL) to extract custom insights and dashboards from Intune data. Implement community-driven solutions, develop custom logs, and utilize Windows Update for Business reports for real-time update monitoring. Gain insights into best practices for building and optimizing Workbooks to elevate your Intune reporting processes.

What you will learn:
 
* Effectively monitor updates using Windows Update for Business reports. 
* Integrate Log Analytics with Intune to enhance reporting capabilities.
* Leverage community-created solutions to expand your reporting potential.
* Create and utilize custom logs for tailored insights that meet your needs.
