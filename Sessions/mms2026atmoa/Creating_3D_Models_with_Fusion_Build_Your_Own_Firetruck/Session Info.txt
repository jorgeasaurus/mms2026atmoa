Session Title: 
Creating 3D Models with Fusion: Build Your Own Firetruck

  Description:
Fusion 360 is a user-friendly CAD program that empowers creativity in 3D printing and woodworking. Discover the fundamental techniques to navigate Fusion as you collaboratively design and fabricate a model firetruck. Gain hands-on experience with essential tools and features that bring your ideas to life, making the intimidating aspects of CAD accessible to everyone, regardless of skill level.

What you will learn:
 
* Learn to navigate Fusion 360 without feeling overwhelmed, unlocking your creative potential. 
* Discover primitive shapes and how to find them, forming the building blocks for your designs.
* Master the use of helpers, sketches, and timelines in Fusion to streamline your workflow.
* Understand the nuances of Boolean operations and explore their various states for complex modeling.
