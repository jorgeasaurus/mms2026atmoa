Session Title: 
Build Your Test Lab: No Lab? No Problem!

  Description:
Creating a dedicated test environment is essential for safely testing software and updates, minimizing risk to production systems. Discover diverse resources for establishing lab environments at home or work, then learn automation methods for rapid, reproducible lab setups that enhance testing efficiency. Streamlined build processes lead to more timely and effective testing without the fear of disrupting production environments.

What you will learn:
 
* Identify local resources to set up a functional lab environment. 
* Leverage cloud solutions for flexible lab capabilities and scalability.
* Automate the lab build process for quick and smooth testing scenarios.
* Implement hydration and point-in-time techniques for effective testing management.
