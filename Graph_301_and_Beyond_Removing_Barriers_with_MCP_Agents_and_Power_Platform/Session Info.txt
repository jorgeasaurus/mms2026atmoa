Session Title: 
Graph 301 and Beyond: Removing Barriers with MCP Agents and Power Platform

  Description:
Elevate Microsoft Graph expertise by diving deeper into operationalizing its capabilities with AI. This advanced session builds on Graph 101 concepts, focusing on understanding the underlying mechanics of agentic AI in workflows. Explore the Microsoft MCP Agent, Lokka, and their integration with Power Platform to unlock innovative possibilities in your environment.

What you will learn:
 
* Leverage MCP Agents in Visual Studio Code to accelerate learning, exploration, and development. 
* Utilize Custom Connectors in Power Platform for low-risk, low-code solutions that simplify AI entry.
* Harness AI as a skill multiplier, enabling a focus on higher-priority tasks and strategic initiatives.
* Discover practical strategies for integrating agentic AI to maximize efficiency and productivity.
